## Brand & Style
This design system establishes a high-caliber, contemporary art gallery and editorial portfolio framework created for Prathamesh More. Rooted deeply in the International Typographic Style (Swiss Style) merged with cutting-edge digital minimalism, the interface treats every viewport as a curated architectural gallery space. 

### Brand Personality & Philosophy
- **Unflinching Precision:** Rigorous structural alignment, mathematical balance, and exacting horizontal and vertical hairline delineations.
- **Editorial Monumentalism:** Dramatic juxtapositions between colossal, tight-tracked display typography and whispers of micro-labeled metadata.
- **Gallery Restraint:** The interface recedes into an impeccably pure canvas of white and tinted warm greys, allowing imagery, typography, and case studies to command total attention.
- **Kinetic Spark:** International Electric Orange is deployed with surgical restraint—acting as a beacon of vitality, directional clarity, and purposeful interaction.

### Emotional Response
Users should experience the quiet authority, confidence, and deliberate air of an exclusive modern design monograph or museum catalog. The emotional resonance is sophisticated, highly cultured, uncompromisingly sharp, and undeniably premium.

## Colors
The color architecture relies on high contrast and tonal purity, strictly operating in light mode.

### Palette Roles & Application
- **Pure White Canvas (`#FFFFFF`):** The primary spatial medium across default body surfaces, core page backgrounds, and card substrates.
- **Gallery Surface (`#F5F5F5`):** A soft, warm neutral stone tint utilized for alternating sectional panels, structural card backdrops, subtle fill chips, and media containment wells.
- **Charcoal Ink (`#111111`):** Deep editorial carbon utilized for primary headline typography, solid statement buttons, prominent icons, and structural focus elements.
- **Muted Carbon (`#666666`):** Balanced mid-grey for secondary body text, chronological indicators, index counters, and supporting descriptors.
- **Delicate Hairline (`#E5E5E5`):** Subtle architectural border line color defining table rows, project card grids, and section dividers without visual clutter.
- **International Electric Orange (`#FF4D00`):** The catalytic accent color. Reserved strictly for active indicators, hover highlights, micro-badges, cursor interactions, and primary interactive focus targets. Never used for large passive backgrounds.

## Typography
Typographic scale is the core compositional vehicle of this system. Powered by **Manrope**, the typographic hierarchy marries geometric modernist forms with humanistic letter proportions.

### Typographic Principles
- **Extreme Scale Jumps:** Dramatic scale contrast establishes instant hierarchy. Enormous display titles (e.g., lowercase kinetic titles like "visual poetry" or section-crossing marquees) contrast directly with ultra-compact numerical tags (e.g., `01`, `02`, `11px` uppercase labels).
- **Tracking & Letter-Spacing Dynamics:** Large headlines feature negative tracking (`-0.035em` to `-0.05em`) to compact character pairs into unified silhouette blocks. In contrast, index numbers, sub-headers, and uppercase category markers use expanded tracking (`+0.05em` to `+0.12em`) for heightened legibility and catalog precision.
- **Editorial Numbers:** Case indices, dates, and metric figures are styled either in bold condensed display weights or with clean zero-padded prefixes (`01`, `02`) to evoke structured inventory lists.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout system is founded on a 12-column Swiss typographic grid structured around mathematical rhythm and deliberate asymmetry.

### Grid Systems & Form Factors
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column responsive grid with a max width of 1440px (or full-bleed edge margins of `3.5rem` for expansive gallery views). Column gutters are fixed to `2rem`.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8-column grid with `1.5rem` gutters and `2rem` screen margins. Multi-column editorial exhibition lists consolidate to alternating horizontal card rows.
- **Mobile (320px – 767px):** 4-column fluid grid with `1rem` gutters and `1.25rem` screen margins. Heavy scale headers shift to mobile-adapted sizes (`display-hero-mobile`), stacked vertical actions, and touch-optimized tap rows.

### Spacing Philosophy
- **Negative Space as Compositional Material:** Sections utilize generous vertical padding (`space-4xl` to `space-5xl`) between thematic segments, ensuring project imagery never feels compressed.
- **Grid Crossings & Micro-alignments:** Captions and project indices align strictly to column coordinates, with micro-borders (`1px`) running continuously across the grid to tether typographic elements to their visual anchors.

## Elevation & Depth
In alignment with modern Swiss graphic design and gallery aesthetics, physical drop shadows and heavy multi-tiered z-depth elevations are avoided. 

### Spatial Layering & Surface Techniques
- **Architectural Micro-Borders:** Planar depth is defined using 1px crisp borders (`#E5E5E5`). Rows, exhibition cells, and portfolio frames utilize hairline borders to delineate bounds with razor-sharp definition.
- **Tonal Inset Substrates:** Visual hierarchy is built through flat surface shifting. Default cards rest directly on the `#FFFFFF` canvas, while recessed details or active project previews sit on tonal `#F5F5F5` backdrops.
- **Subtle Ambient Focus Shadow (Interactive Only):** When a project card or floating action button requires elevation on user interaction, an imperceptible, highly diffused ambient halo is applied: `box-shadow: 0 16px 36px -12px rgba(17, 17, 17, 0.08)`. This adds weightless buoyancy without breaking the flat editorial aesthetic.
- **Micro-Hairline Overlays:** Floating action pills and navigation bars apply an ultra-subtle frosted glass layer (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` over `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.88)`) with a `1px solid rgba(229, 229, 229, 0.6)` border.

## Shapes
The shape language combines crisp architectural rectangles with deliberately selected rounded geometry to achieve a balance between Swiss structure and modern warmth.

### Geometry Specifications
- **Structural Project Frames:** Primary showcase containers, media framing modules, and portrait viewports feature a confident `1rem` (`16px`) radius (`rounded-lg`), softening monumental imagery without turning playful.
- **Nested Card Modules:** Child elements, nested badges, and media insets within cards use `0.5rem` (`8px`) radius (`rounded-md`).
- **Pill Badges & Circular Orbitals:** Status tags, round social buttons, floating navigational triggers, and arrow tokens employ a full circular radius (`9999px` / `rounded-full`). This contrast between strict horizontal/vertical lines and smooth pills reinforces the contemporary gallery aesthetic.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary Editorial Button:** Solid `#111111` background, `#FFFFFF` text, `rounded-full`, `height: 48px`, horizontal padding `28px`. Font: `label-index`. Hover: Transforms to `#FF4D00` with subtle outward scale (`scale(1.02)`), maintaining crisp contrast.
- **Secondary Ghost / Outline Button:** Hairline border (`1px solid #111111` or `#E5E5E5`), transparent background, `#111111` text, `rounded-full`. Hover: Background fills with `#F5F5F5` or flips to solid `#111111` with `#FFFFFF` text.
- **Orbital Arrow Button:** Circular icon button (`48px x 48px`), `rounded-full`, border `1px solid #E5E5E5` or filled `#111111`. Features an angled arrow (`↗`). Hover: Rotates to `45deg` upward with accent orange ring.

### Exhibition & Index List Row
- **Structure:** Horizontal layout spanning full grid width with continuous `1px solid #E5E5E5` top and bottom dividers.
- **Content Flow:** 
  1. Sequential index label (e.g., `01`, `02`) in `label-index` (`#666666`).
  2. Exhibition title in `headline-md` italic/bold (`#111111`).
  3. Location and date metadata in `body-sm` (`#666666`).
  4. Interactive terminal element: Outline pill button or directional arrow.
- **State Behavior:** Row hover transitions background to `#F5F5F5` with swift, smooth easing (`200ms ease-out`), title shifting slightly right (`+8px`).

### Portfolio & Project Cards
- **Card Framing:** Enclosed within `rounded-lg` (`16px`) borders with `1px solid #E5E5E5`.
- **Imagery Canvas:** Visual content is presented full-bleed within the upper card area with a subtle `overflow: hidden` mask. Aspect ratios strictly follow `4:5`, `1:1`, or `16:10` editorial standards.
- **Metadata Block:** Bottom container with project category tags (`label-caps`), title (`headline-sm`), and year of execution.

### Chips & Category Filters
- **Pill Form:** Capsule shape (`rounded-full`), height `32px`, padding `4px 16px`.
- **Default State:** Background `#F5F5F5`, text `#666666`, border `1px solid transparent`.
- **Active State:** Background `#111111`, text `#FFFFFF`, with an optional mini dot in `#FF4D00` preceding the text.

### Form Inputs & Contact Fields
- **Minimal Hairline Inputs:** Borderless top/sides with an authoritative bottom border (`1.5px solid #E5E5E5`). Background is transparent.
- **Typography:** Placeholder text rendered in `body-md` (`#666666`).
- **Focus State:** Bottom border illuminates to `#FF4D00` or `#111111`, paired with a crisp transition and floating uppercase label in `label-caps`.

### Marquee & Monumental Typography Tickers
- **Application:** Continuous typographic banner ribbons (e.g., "about . about . about ." or project disciplines) running horizontally.
- **Styling:** Giant uppercase or lowercase outline/solid text using `headline-xl` or `display-hero`, tracking `-0.04em`, anchored with hairline horizontal rules.
